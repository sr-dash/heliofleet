Lines of sight from each instrument are followed to where they cross the plane through Sun centre perpendicular to the line from the Sun to the chosen observer. Earth and the spacecraft appear where that observer sees them. MLSO and DKIST fields are traced from Earth in the same way. Fields are drawn from the published elongation ranges; heliospheric imagers use the published field sizes.
